## 1. Deadline boundary

The official ICLR 2027 deadlines are:

- genuine abstract: 2026-09-18 23:59 AoE;
- full paper: 2026-09-25 23:59 AoE;
- main text: at most nine pages, excluding references;
- double-blind submission and mandatory AI-use statement.

The abstract must describe the result that actually exists by September 18. New authors cannot be
added after that deadline. A placeholder abstract is not admissible.

## 3. What “fast” means

The following may be accelerated:

- reuse immutable outputs and model caches after exact identity checks;
- implement and test executors before compute becomes available;
- use safe resumable checkpoints and deduplicate shared subset/edge evaluations;
- short-circuit a neutralizer after a valid harmful witness, where exactness is preserved;
- run documentation, bibliography, analysis code, and paper infrastructure alongside compute;
- apply predeclared gates immediately so failed routes do not consume confirmatory resources.

The following may not be omitted or weakened because of time:

- fresh confirmatory separation;
- payload invariance and outcome-blind unitization;
- both primary neutralizers and common sampling seeds;
- full-response panel evaluation with abstention;
- strict-subset checks without a monotonicity assumption;
- truncation, malformed-input, and capability-confound handling;
- fresh-seed replication of reported minimal edges and their strict subsets;
- required one-unit and one-path baselines;
- payload-clustered uncertainty and complete denominator reporting;
- model/data/runtime provenance, anonymization, ethics, and AI-use disclosure.

Only optional extensions may be dropped: optimized-suffix families, white-box attribution,
canonical BF16 edge transfer, extra neutralizers, or a newly recruited human study. Dropping these
requires the title and claims to remain explicitly scoped to the completed Q4 structured-semantic
study.

## 8. Decision discipline

- A failed early gate saves time by preventing an invalid larger experiment; it does not remove a
  required test from a successful route.
- If D3 fails, do not spend confirmation compute hoping for a better result.
- If C1 lacks the minimum fresh population, do not reinterpret development examples as fresh.
- If C2 shows only obvious singleton topology, do not promote engineering completeness to C3.
- If C3 replication, neutralizer agreement, capability, or baseline-gap criteria fail, narrow or
  stop the claim rather than alter the rules.
- If the complete evidence chain cannot close by the genuine-abstract deadline, do not submit a
  placeholder or unverifiable ICLR claim.
