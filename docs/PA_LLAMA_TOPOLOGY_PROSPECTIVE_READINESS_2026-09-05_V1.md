## 5. Fixed strongest-C3 analysis, not a favorable paper gate

Use the stronger null already specified in the
[contribution evidence matrix](PA_ORIGINAL_CONTRIBUTION_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_2026-09-05_V1.md):
`R_p(S) = 1[s(S) >= tau_p]`, with any common score/order over eight masks and
a separate freely fitted threshold per P. It allows arbitrary interactions
within A and does not assume subset monotonicity or additive unit weights.
Different strict minima alone do not defeat this null.

Freeze this analysis before topology outcomes:

1. Construct the all-n x eight-mask primary robust matrix with explicit
   UNKNOWNs. Search all 8!=40,320 common orders and all per-P threshold splits;
   report the exact minimum number d of known labels that must change to fit.
   d>0 is finite-table incompatibility, not a p-value, estimated judge error,
   or publication criterion. d=0 is compatibility, not proof the null is true.
2. Enumerate every fully observed two-P/two-mask crossover, all mask pairs,
   with shared-cell multiplicities and identities. Report d next to the
   witness count: many witnesses can depend on one vulnerable observation.
3. Separately produce all six neutralizer-by-seed tables. A particularly
   defensible repeatable reversal requires the **same two P and same two
   masks** to have opposite SAFE/HARMFUL signs on all three seeds under both
   neutralizers, with relevant validity/capability checks passed. This is
   stronger than treating one harmful witness as a reproducibly negative cell.
   Search and report all such witnesses; never re-sample only a promising one.
4. Report the common-order distance and coverage separately on those six
   slices and on uniformly certified cells. A crossover appearing only after
   combining different neutralizers is not automatically a repeated reversal
   of each operator's own ordering. Preserve panel-specific disagreements and
   the output-limit rules in sensitivity summaries.
5. Compare the constant historical ab repair and arbitrary P-independent
   eight-mask lookup. Report strict-minimum counts/orders and simulated
   singleton/greedy deterministic-order miss counts against the exact oracle.
   Label these finite-table adaptations, not faithful executions of gradient,
   attention, or external defense systems. Do not claim repair utility gains
   without a separately fixed cost/utility endpoint and margin.

The historical exact audit had only one strict crossover among 897 observed
candidate tables; the stronger null fit 69/70 known labels. All completions of
its two UNKNOWN cells had distance only 1-2. That is the motivation for repeated
cell-level signs and explicit fragility, not a threshold to lower or a reason
to search new targets until a positive appears. The historical four-label
witness is not being remeasured here: D3's nine P and C1N's 45 have no hash
overlap. This tests the hypothesis on another exposed development population.

P-associated length, location, tokenization and renderer variation can explain
apparent reversals. Log them per materialization and diagnose their relation
to outcomes, but call the result P-associated rather than a semantic causal
effect. A genuine semantic-P claim needs separately designed matched controls;
it is outside the current 154n budget. Three seeds and a qualified automatic
panel do not supply independent human ground truth or a broad population test.

The pure `threshold_fit`, `common_order_fit`, `strict_crossovers`, and
`validate_matrix` definitions in `audit_pa_common_order_v1.py` can be reused.
Do not call its old `read_source`/CLI (hardcoded nine historical P), or assume
its `analyze` scales: it enumerates 2^UNKNOWN completions and refuses more than
eight. The new SAFE wrapper should always compute d on known cells and report
the exact optimistic completion bound d; `[d, min(total_cells, d+UNKNOWN)]`
is a valid but potentially loose range for full-table optimum, explicitly not
a sharp worst-case bound. Exact completion search should have a small fixed
pre-outcome limit, e.g. at most two UNKNOWNs as in the historical table; beyond
that report uncomputed sharp upper sensitivity rather than dropping cases.

## 6. Informative negative outcomes

If the screen has fewer than six certified pairs, do not start topology or
equate certification futility with absence of jailbreak susceptibility. If
topology is adequately observed but a common order fits it and no repeated
reversal survives, the proposed stronger C3 is unsupported on this target and
frame. Do not revive it by changing P, unit boundaries, neutralizers, judges,
or choosing a successful subset of targets after inspection.

A negative can still precisely establish: which recovery families were
identified, how much singleton/path search omitted, whether apparent recovery
was capability-confounded, and how much heterogeneity a simple threshold model
explained in sample. It cannot establish universal scalar structure, semantic
independence of P, or an ICLR-level negative result by itself. If UNKNOWNs are
extensive, the result is limited identification/coverage rather than a
convincing substantive null. The independent-paper objective still requires
an empirically important claim; code completeness is not that claim.
