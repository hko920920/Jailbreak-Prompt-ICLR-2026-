## Why the generic mathematical escape routes are insufficient

The AND aggregation preserves a common ordering of underlying probabilities
under equal-size iid repetition; it does not systematically reverse that order.
Nevertheless, finite samples can form a checkerboard. For illustration only,
give two identical P independent Bernoulli recovery probabilities q(a)=0.8,
q(b)=0.9. Six-success probabilities are0.262144 and0.531441; a specified checkerboard
orientation has probability0.0481649236. This is not this experiment's error rate.

A deterministic realization makes the distinction concrete: slice1 has
P1=(1,0),P2=(1,1); slice2 P1=(1,1),P2=(0,1); slices3–6 both=(1,1).
Each slice separately fits some common order; the AND aggregate is a checkerboard.
If every realized slice instead shared the SAME deterministic order, AND would
preserve it. This is elementary sampling/aggregation reasoning, not a novel theorem
or proof that sampling caused the real Stage7 exceptions.

Similarly, in an illustrative iid one-cell experiment, three successes give a
one-sided95% exact lower success-probability bound of0.05^(1/3)=0.3684. Do not
pool two different operators or call this an actual calibrated study interval.
Even59 all-success iid observations are needed for that one-cell lower bound
to exceed0.95; a simple336-cell Bonferroni illustration needs172 per cell. These
are arithmetic examples, not a selected sample size or a claim no better design
exists. A low-risk deployment certificate cannot be promised from three seeds.

Two judges with unidentified joint error can produce the same SAFE observations
under different latent human-safety states. Repetition then estimates the proxy
more precisely; it does not identify truth. Genuine safety-error guarantees need
relevant external reference evidence/error assumptions. Under the existing no-new-
human constraint, either remain explicitly panel-relative or propose a separately
approved exact-checkable security endpoint. Neither is automatically sufficient
for a main paper. The old necessary/possible-family formulas already exist in
[the rescue theory audit](RESCUE_THEORY_AND_NOVELTY_AUDIT_2026-09-05_V2.md);
do not rebrand them as a new contribution.
