# V4 prospective transfer validation: completed result -- 2026-09-05

## Outcome and scope

The new-input validation reproduced output-contract failures after changing a certified
minimal repair from BLANK to OMIT. The predeclared primary joint event occurred in 65/128
stratum/problem opportunities (50.78125%), covering 38 of 64 underlying problem clusters.
This is a positive fixed-template new-input result, not restoration of the failed D3/C1N
claims, proof of a strong paper, or a safety result. Most qualifying failures are formatting
failures, not established errors in the underlying task computation.

The experiment and its pre-output analysis contract completed unchanged. All 1,120 unique
requests have durable replies; all 1,280 planned logical views are retained; UNKNOWN is zero.
Independent safe-row reconstruction agrees with the frozen analyzer. A separate post-freeze
QA replayed every saved harmless reply through the frozen parser and task scorer: 1,120/1,120
agree with all public logical views. No experimental label was changed during QA.

The author's requests to continue rescue, preserve detailed records, report before experiments
and stop manuscript/PDF work remain respected. No original Primary A or Reserve B cohort was
used. The V1 authority document still matches its original hash.

## Frozen design and denominators

See the immutable [pre-output protocol](RESCUE_V4_PROSPECTIVE_TRANSFER_PROTOCOL_2026-09-05.md)
and append-only [execution log](RESCUE_V4_LIVE_EXECUTION_LOG_2026-09-05.md).

The four primary masks were selected from the V3 discovery results, not from V4. V4 generated
32 new lookup and 32 new sorting problems before target output. Lookup dictionaries and
queries jointly define a problem. Sorting identity is the unordered triple; reordering does
not create a new independent problem. The existing templates, note spans, two quantized
models, native chat framing, seed 11, greedy settings and output cap were unchanged.

Every applicable problem remains in all five strata. There was no screening, outcome-based
exclusion, replacement, template adjustment or additional seed claim. The 160 stratum/problem
views comprise 128 model/problem views over 64 semantic problems; these are not interchangeable
sample sizes. The two Qwen sorting masks share observations. Exactly 160 requests have two
logical views; 960 have one, giving 1,120 requests and 1,280 logical rows.

For each fixed source mask S, the primary event requires all three normal controls correct,
BLANK(S) correct, every proper subset BLANK(T) incorrect, and OMIT(S) incorrect. The unedited
case is among the proper subsets. This verifies strict inclusion minimality, not minimum
cardinality. V4 does not measure all target-operator proper subsets or the complete five-unit
minimal family. A correct transferred response does not certify target-operator minimality.

## What failed in the outputs

Among the 65 primary qualified failures, 60 Qwen responses are MALFORMED_JSON and five Gemma
responses are WRONG_VALUE under the frozen exact-output scorer. MALFORMED_JSON does not
establish that the underlying sorted answer is wrong. The defensible claim is output-contract
non-transfer, mainly involving formatting, not a general reasoning failure or harmfulness.

Across all measured target-witness rows, Qwen has 63 malformed responses (including three
without a qualifying source repair); Gemma lookup has 29 wrong values (only five qualify as
failed transfers); Gemma sorting has six wrong values (two qualify). All these observations
remain in the safe result. Model comparisons are confounded with conflict style: Qwen uses
format conflicts and Gemma value conflicts.

The full row totals are 684 CORRECT, 596 INCORRECT, zero UNKNOWN. Unique-request totals are
588 CORRECT, 532 INCORRECT. These mix intentionally failing proper subsets with controls and
witnesses and must not be advertised as a model benchmark accuracy score.

## Uncertainty and verification

Because no required observation is UNKNOWN, the primary fixed-panel identification bounds
collapse to [0.5078125, 0.5078125]. This is not a zero-width confidence interval. The frozen
10,000-draw paired problem-cluster sensitivity calculation gives [0.453125, 0.5625], labelled
APPROXIMATE_GENERATOR_SENSITIVITY, not nominal population coverage. It preserves lookup-key
position balance and shared model/mask indices. The panel uses fixed templates and a partly
without-replacement generator; there is no independent stochastic-seed replication.

Before dispatch: 85 focused tests and Ruff passed. After completion: the independently
implemented safe-row audit rehashed all row/request identities and reconstructed every event
without importing the statistical analyzer. Post-freeze receipt QA has 13 synthetic tests,
including tampering and UNKNOWN cases, and Ruff passed. It verified journal identities,
receipt commitments, raw-reply metadata, exact parser/gold replay and all duplicate views.
Elapsed time was checked for metadata consistency, not independently remeasured.

The local freeze is not externally timestamped preregistration. Self-hashes demonstrate
internal consistency, not independent authorship or hardware attestation. Native chat-template
digests match V3 in separate ownership receipts but are not attached row-wise to an execution
instance. Greedy decoding does not establish repeat-run GPU determinism. Public safe hashes
alone do not allow outsiders to replay raw response scoring; no harmless reply export has
been published, and no historical private response is part of this study's QA.

## Research decision, not a manuscript claim

There is now prospective new-input evidence for the narrow operator-transfer finding. It is
not yet a demonstrated practically useful or novel paper contribution. The independent
review notes that removal specifications and formal minimal explanations are established,
and a destination-only correctness check already detects the present transfer failure.
Full minimality certification needs value beyond that single recheck.

A more concrete followup candidate is to test whether irreversible singleton-failure pruning
in utility-preserving prompt sanitization causes unnecessary information retention. The rule
is present in the [official implementation at pinned commit 6163199](https://github.com/PEACH-Research-Lab/Operationalize-Data-Minimization/blob/6163199e42a2fd5c9415a6607fd69042c53f902e/run_pipeline.py#L841)
and was independently checked in the
[final ICLR 2026 paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/5d77373490c2afe9cb1529bc455d6efc-Paper-Conference.pdf).
The authors already acknowledge utility-preserving predictions outperforming their oracle;
generic non-optimality is therefore not a new discovery. The narrower proposed question must
isolate irreversible pruning from comparator error, stopping, decoding variability and
measurement error. No published empirical rate has been refuted by this study.

No followup experiment is claimed completed here, and V4 is not retroactively evidence for
that new primary question. A future study needs an explicit task/utility/retention definition,
fixed public-data selection, fair no-freeze and exact-oracle comparisons, feasible call budget
and its own pre-output protocol. Manuscript/PDF work remains inactive.
