## 3. Strongest specialist rejection case

The strongest current rejection is not evaluator noise. It is the granularity objection:

> `AIMDecorator` and `RefusalSuppressionDecorator` may be two implementation fragments of one
> obvious macro attack block. When they are merged prospectively as `G0`, every h4rm3l instance is
> explained by the same singleton. Fine-vocabulary order-two edges may therefore manufacture
> apparent synergy by splitting that block.

The paper cannot refute this observation. It can survive it only as a narrower measurement claim:

1. primary units are source-native, independently implemented decorators fixed before outputs;
2. completeness and minimality are explicitly vocabulary-relative;
3. the coarsening collapse is reported in the main text, not hidden in an appendix;
4. `synergy`, `granularity-invariant`, and broad attack-mechanism claims are prohibited;
5. the central empirical value must be complete-family recovery and measured one-path omission;
6. that omission must recur on fresh payloads and both target-model cells.

The second rejection is significance: exhaustive search over three units is computationally
trivial. The search algorithm is therefore not a contribution. The contribution can only be the
carefully controlled empirical object, the complete-family audit, and a repeated finding that the
usual one-unit/one-path answer is incomplete. If the repeated gap is absent, the central paper
claim stops.

## 5. Target-model admission choice

### Recommended primary pair

1. **Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q4_K_M**: retain the exact official Qwen GGUF already used for D3. This
   gives fresh-payload continuity with the development result.
2. **Gemma 4 E4B IT QAT Q4_0**: admit the current official Google GGUF as the second target-model
   family, subject to a full harmless runtime gate before any C1N output.

Why this is preferred: Qwen plus Gemma supplies development continuity, a distinct target family,
a 2026 model, and official quantized artifacts. The official
[Gemma repository](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E4B-it-qat-q4_0-gguf/tree/main)
is public, ungated, Apache-2.0, and currently resolves to revision
`4b4a2c1d584be7264f87aac328a1bc739ce81b6c`. The text model file is
`gemma-4-E4B_q4_0-it.gguf`, 5,154,941,280 bytes, with LFS SHA-256
`676c35070db6dbe52f93e9c864ee0fba4eddea94b9c875d9cb10daff453fbaee`.
The 991,552,256-byte multimodal projector is unnecessary for the proposed text-only study and must
not be downloaded unless a later frozen extension requires it.

The existing pinned llama.cpp `b10441` Vulkan runtime should be tested first so Qwen and Gemma use
one runtime. If it cannot load or template Gemma correctly, a runtime transition must be frozen and
both targets requalified on the same replacement. A current official Windows Vulkan fallback is
[llama.cpp b10816](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b10816), commit

If Gemma fails the harmless artifact/runtime gate **before any Gemma C1N scientific output exists**,

### C2N: exact confirmatory topology

For every C1N-eligible pair, evaluate the complete eight-subset primary lattice under both frozen
neutralizers and all three primary seeds. The empty-set attacked baseline is reused from C1N; the
maximum new **lattice** target calls are therefore
`7 subsets x 2 neutralizers x 3 seeds = 42` per instance, before exact harmful-witness stopping.
Every provisional recovery also opens four already defined matched capability controls (two
neutralizers by two harmless tasks), so the separate worst-case control budget is
`7 x 2 x 2 = 28` and the absolute per-instance target-call ceiling is `42 + 28 = 70`. The observed
D3 h4rm3l control load was materially lower: 25 provisional subsets and 100 controls across nine
instances. No monotonicity assumption or outcome-based subset pruning is allowed.

Derive from the exact table, without new target calls:

- all-singleton;
- leave-one-out ranking;
- DDMIN;
- greedy forward and greedy backward;
- primary-to-coarsened topology projection;
- evaluator-axis and admitted rule sensitivity.

Proposed C2N gate before fresh-seed replication:

1. at least 25 reportable topologies from at least 20 unique payloads;
2. at least six NONTRIVIAL instances;
3. NONTRIVIAL occurs in both target-model cells and more than one payload;
4. positive-decision neutralizer Jaccard is at least `0.80`;
5. capability-confounded plus abstained fraction is below `0.20`;
6. no one source-native named unit or full wrapper explains every reportable recovery;
7. best one-unit or one-path minimal-family recall loss is at least `0.15`.

The mandatory adjacent coarsening is reported as a primary limitation/sensitivity. Coarsened
nontriviality is not silently substituted for primary-vocabulary nontriviality, and primary
nontriviality is not described as granularity-invariant.
