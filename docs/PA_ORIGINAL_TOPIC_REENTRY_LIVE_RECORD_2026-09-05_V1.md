#### Structural result: coarse sameness does not imply identical fine structure

All nine h4rm3l instances remain included: 72 subset decisions, comprising
25 RECOVERED, 45 NOT_RECOVERED and two ABSTAINED. Seven complete tables have
four distinct fully observed minimal-family profiles:

| Fine minimal-family profile | Complete-table instances |
| --- | ---: |
| {a} | 1 |
| {a,b} only | 4 |
| {a,b} and {a,c} | 1 |
| {a,b}, {a,c} and {b,c} | 1 |

Here a, b and c denote the three fixed source units in the artifact vocabulary;
this table describes sets to neutralize, not sufficient attack-generating sets.
The two partial tables are NOT dropped or assigned to a preferred profile:
one permits the last two listed profiles; the other permits the first or third.

Across 36 within-frame payload-pair comparisons, 25 have known differences,
six have identical complete profiles and five remain unresolved. The 36 pairs
are dependent comparisons of nine development instances, not a sample size.
There is no common possible strict-minimum set across all nine instances.

The strongest simple repair remains intact: removing the previously defined
macro G0={a,b} succeeds in 9/9 cases. It is strict-minimal in seven, definitely
not minimal in one and unresolved in one. All nine coarse tables are identical.
Thus fine explanation diversity does not establish better defense performance.
Even an oracle selecting minimum-cardinality repairs saves only 1-2 source units
in total relative to removing 18 units across the nine cases; this is not a
token-cost or task-utility measurement.

The stricter hypothetical explanation "recovery iff G0 is removed" contradicts
seven known fine-scale decisions across five instances. It is explicitly a
diagnostic explanation rule, not a guarantee attributed to the simple repair.
