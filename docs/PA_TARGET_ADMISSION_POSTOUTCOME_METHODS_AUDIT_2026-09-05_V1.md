## Decision in brief

The original P-fixed/A-unit recovery-topology topic remains primary. Llama V2
remains an independently verified 7/10 FAIL, and Llama is not admitted. The
recorded three word-plus-period failures diagnose an admission-endpoint relevance
problem, not a new failure of the paper's empirical hypothesis.

Another run of the same ten trivia/format fixtures is not scientifically
necessary. Existing V2 receipts already establish bounded runtime identity,
complete request/response transport, and the one tested fresh-process replay.
They do not establish the intended stochastic, longer, free-response operating
condition, general capability, judge validity, jailbreak yield, or topology.

Recommend ONE route: draft a six-request prospective DEVELOPMENT regime-transfer
check: both historically fixed D3 harmless control task types, each at the three
research seeds. If subsequently authorized, run its one fixed batch and close
it, regardless of outcome. Do not
reopen V2, rescore its replies into admission, repeat until passing, or cycle
through new targets. This is an optional, justified engineering qualification
for a changed operating condition, not a logically necessary scientific test
of the P/A hypothesis or an independent confirmatory result.

## Administrative qualification is not statistical confirmation

The finite V2 capability gate asks whether a particular runtime/target satisfies
a declared set of operational checks. Its failure must remain visible and is
not a p-value, estimated scientific effect, or proof of general incapability.
Changing an administrative specification is not automatically statistical
misconduct. It becomes misleading if the change is concealed, described as the
original pass, justified only by the candidate's known failures, or used to
claim independent scientific confirmation.

There are three different facts to preserve simultaneously:

1. V2 correctly failed its frozen exact-content/format requirement.
2. The original research endpoint does not require punctuation-free single-word
   target answers; word content and exact formatting were unnecessarily joined.
3. Learning this mismatch from V2 outputs makes any new design result-informed.
   Its motivation cannot be relabelled as wholly pre-output merely by creating
   a new file, version, fixture bank, or later timestamp.

New fixtures can prevent direct reuse of the same answers as qualification
evidence. They do not erase model-selection history, remembered response style,
researcher discretion, or the result-informed choice of scoring construct.
Record those facts and restrict the new claim to development setup. Do not
calculate a punctuation-tolerant "10/10" on the old answers and use it as an
admission result, even if accompanied by a disclaimer elsewhere.

## Is another harmless qualification necessary?

Not for rediscovering that the pinned model loads, that the native template is
the source-derived one, that ten short requests return, or that the sentinel
replays. Those are already measured operational facts despite V2's failed
aggregate gate. Rechecking hashes before a new launch is ordinary integrity
hygiene, not a reason to repeat an entire empirical qualification programme.

A limited new check can have a distinct purpose: exercise the intended
stochastic/free-response path at the research settings instead of seed 17,
temperature 0 and a 48-token cap. This motivates a single, small integration
and functional-capability check. It does not make such a batch statistically
indispensable; it must not be sold as an additional research contribution.

The cap is an upper bound, not an instruction to force 512 output tokens.
Three seeds on a few harmless tasks are repeated operating-condition probes,
not independent evidence of stable jailbreak behavior or robust recovery.
Do not require equality across different seeds, or infer general determinism
from these probes. Judge agreement on harmless responses would not establish
coverage on later attack-conditioned responses; no new judge check is proposed.

The current re-entry record explicitly requires qualification before a new
screen. A relevance argument alone does not waive that authorization boundary.
The root must publish the replacement DEVELOPMENT qualification purpose and
decision rule prospectively, with the post-outcome motivation disclosed. This
audit does not itself approve the target or launch the next screen.
Calling a replacement rule "readiness" instead of "admission" does not solve
this issue: if it changes which evidence permits development to proceed, that
substantive administrative change must be declared explicitly. A rename is
not permission to hide that the earlier required all-ten criterion failed.

## Requirements for the one prospective draft

The concrete root proposal reviewed here has exactly six future requests:
the original P2 arithmetic text and original P2 copy-token text, each at seeds
11, 23 and 47. It selects ALL task types from the pre-Llama D3 control list,
not a discretionary subset chosen from the V2 successes. It retains V2's
integer and exact-text scoring semantics unchanged. No word scorer is involved,
no punctuation tolerance is added, and no old response is rescored.

This is a defensible narrow relevance correction IF the draft preserves the
following disclosures and limitations:

- Both selected task types were already observed to pass V2. Their historical
  selection is an independent provenance constraint, not proof that the new
  study was designed without knowledge of the outcomes.
- The new information is execution and exact task success under the new
  stochastic settings. Six results are two tasks with three seed repetitions,
  not six independent capabilities or an estimated population success rate.
- Bare harmless tasks are not the structure-matched intervention controls in
  D3. They cannot replace later per-neutralizer/per-intervention capability
  controls or demonstrate capability preservation after editing attack units.
- These short tasks do not test long outputs, long context, complex reasoning,
  full-response safety, evaluator agreement, or recovery. A 512-token cap
  alone establishes none of those properties.
- A 6/6 result may be labelled only a passed bare-control stochastic-regime
  check. A new development-only readiness decision is administrative and
  conditional, not general Llama capability admission, original V2 admission,
  unchanged Step5N admission, or new scientific confirmation.

The source-derived selection rule, exact six-request list, task-specific answer
rules, all generation settings, total request ceiling, one-shot stop rule and
claim limits must be fixed before new output. The all-six criterion is a
finite execution/functional requirement for that listed plan, not a statistically
calibrated adequacy threshold. Any failure or unresolved request fails this
limited profile; it does not trigger a smaller denominator or a seventh call.

Use the research settings only as a new operating contract, not as permission
to reopen C1N: temperature 0.7, seeds 11/23/47, maximum 512 new tokens, and the
remaining settings fixed explicitly. Keep model, tokenizer/template, runtime,
sampling order, extraction and output disposition fixed across the batch.

Separate three axes:

- Operational integrity: exact identity, correct request/response binding,
  successful complete termination, no ambiguous retry, resource-safe execution
  and cleanup. These requirements protect usable measurements.
- Functional content: objectively specified task success with whole-answer
  validation that rejects wrong values, contradiction and unrelated content.
  Do not restore arbitrary substring credit. A whole-answer grammar must be
  justified by the task, not just be a more elaborate exact-format trap.
- Required formatting: enforce it only when the actual task or downstream
  consumer needs it. The original target endpoint has no blanket exact-JSON,
  ASCII-only, or punctuation-free requirement. An optional formatting diagnostic
  must stay separate and cannot silently reappear in a required all-pass gate.

For this concrete six-call proposal, the exact answer rules are already V2
rules and should be checked for unchanged identity rather than redesigned.
The numeric task explicitly asks for only its number and the copy task asks
for exact reproduction, so formatting has a task-specific rationale here.
Their tests should still reject a larger incorrect number containing the
expected digits, unrelated prose, or a changed token. This does not justify
reinstating arbitrary substring checks for any future scientific control.

Any later proposed different fixture count, error-tolerant routing, or
threshold would require a separately stated operational rationale. There is no scientific
theorem that ten trivia questions, or ten out of ten, is necessary or sufficient
for this free-response study. Neither reducing the denominator nor changing
the threshold is allowed after the new batch is observed.

## Closure and stop rules

The recommended route is ONE predeclared DEVELOPMENT functional-check batch,
not an open-ended qualification campaign. No model generation is authorized
by this audit or by a draft alone.

For any subsequently authorized batch:

1. Execute the fixed plan once. Record every attempted request and every failed
   outcome; no best-seed selection or omission of inconvenient fixtures.
2. Stop on transport/integrity ambiguity and preserve the partial attempt.
   Do not resend an ambiguous request. A partial batch cannot qualify.
3. Use the declared rule for content failures and final disposition. Do not
   change scorer, formatting tolerance, threshold, model or seed after output.
4. Close the package after that disposition. No automatic second batch,
   replacement-target cycle, new evaluator or exception that makes it pass.
5. A pass would authorize consideration of a SEPARATELY frozen all-45 exposed-P
   DEVELOPMENT screen, not original C2N, sealed cohorts or a paper claim.
   A failure would trigger a finite evidence report and explicit research-plan
   decision, not endless infrastructure work or a claim that P/A is disproved.

The next scientifically valuable evidence remains stable P-associated fine
recovery structure under the declared controls and adequate panel coverage.
Administrative checks must stay subordinate to that question. This audit
performs zero calls and earns no new structural, measurement or paper result.
