# D1 Major-Gate Decision Options — 2026-09-04 V1

Status: **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED; NO FURTHER MODEL INFERENCE AUTHORIZED**

## Decision fact

D1 completed correctly but found zero E0G5-certified stable pairs. Six of eight pairs are
unresolved because the selective two-axis panel abstained in at least one of the six required
direct/attacked seed cells. Two DeepInception pairs have conclusive attacked-safe witnesses. D2 is
therefore closed.

This is not evidence that the topology hypothesis is false. It is evidence that the current
four-payload P3 population and strict selective measurement system cannot jointly supply an
admissible topology instance.

## Option A — prospective measurement-power repair and one broader fresh screen

Recommendation: **best remaining scientific route, if the author chooses to continue.**

Do not relabel D1. Freeze a new versioned development-screen contract before any new generation.
Retain the E0G5 panel, three-seed stability predicate, immutable payload, and all safety boundaries.
Use a larger fresh payload denominator and a prospectively selected second attack family so that
selective per-record coverage is not mistaken for six-cell pair-level power. Treat D1 openly as a
negative pilot used to budget the new development screen. Keep later confirmation disjoint.

Required guardrails:

1. freeze the exact payload source, count, attack families, target models, seeds, prompts, and stop
   rule before generation;
2. derive the required screen size from conservative lower-bound direct-safe and attacked-harmful
   rates rather than choosing a round number;
3. do not use one-axis labels, weakened seed rules, historical WildGuard selection, or post-hoc
   human resolution;
4. stop the empirical route if the one allowed fresh screen again yields no admissible population;
5. preserve Steps 3--10 only if their original gates are actually reached.

Provisional cost: 45--90 minutes to design, power-bound, implement, and preflight the new contract;
approximately 1--3 local-GPU hours for a moderate fresh screen, with the exact runtime stated only
after its denominator is frozen.

Main risk: this is an outcome-informed development redesign. It remains defensible only with full
disclosure and a genuinely fresh later confirmation set.

## Option B — replace or augment the evaluator

Recommendation: **not preferred with three weeks remaining.**

A stronger-coverage judge could be externally calibrated and frozen, but it must be qualified on
data independent of P3 and then used on fresh responses. It cannot retroactively convert D1's 13
abstentions into labels. This repeats the project's longest and highest-risk workstream and may
consume the remaining schedule without testing topology.

A new human annotation route has the same prospective-only constraint and reintroduces the
recruitment, protocol, and reproducibility problems already identified. Selecting Qwen3Guard alone
because it is favorable on h4rm3l is explicitly invalid.

## Option C — stop the empirical submission route

This is the cleanest choice if no additional development screen is acceptable. Preserve D1 as a
negative feasibility result and do not manufacture a topology paper from unresolved cases. A later
project could revisit the hypothesis with a measurement system designed for conjunction-level
coverage.

## Recommended author decision

Choose **Option A** only once, with a predeclared stop if it fails. It offers a bounded way to learn
whether the topic still has an empirical population while preserving the paper's central rules.
Do not start Step 3 directly. If the author rejects this one bounded repair, choose Option C rather
than Option B's open-ended evaluator redevelopment.
