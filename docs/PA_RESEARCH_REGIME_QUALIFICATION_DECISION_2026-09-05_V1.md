### Fixed acceptance and stopping rules for the proposal

An eventual smoke result could pass only with all six requests accounted for,
all six task checks correct, all six replies normally stopped, verified model/
runtime/template/request identity and owned-process cleanup. Missing, truncated,
unparseable or ambiguous results cannot pass. This is an engineering acceptance
choice, not a statistical adequacy theorem.

Complete the remaining planned tasks after a content/format failure, within the
six-call ceiling, so the denominator cannot shrink. Stop immediately on transport
or integrity failure; do not resend an ambiguous request. There is one batch
only. No seventh call, changed seed, changed scorer, replacement task, new target
or relaxed threshold is automatically authorized after a failure. Report any
failure and make an explicit research-plan decision instead of continuing an
open-ended qualification campaign.

### What a pass could and could not mean

The only positive label would be a passed **bare-control stochastic-regime
smoke**. It would allow consideration of a separately implemented and frozen
development screen, not trigger one automatically. It would not qualify Llama
as a judge, establish general capability, prove behavior at long contexts or
512-token answers, certify jailbreak success, demonstrate topology, count as
cross-model replication or support an ICLR contribution.

The existing unwrapped harmless tasks are not the structure-matched benign
copies used for intervention capability controls. Those must still be run at
their declared point of use. Per-response full-output, validity and truncation
checks, the unchanged conservative evaluator panel and ABSTAIN handling also
remain necessary. The 45 exposed C1N payloads would be DEVELOPMENT only; no
fresh-confirmation or A60/B60 access is included.
