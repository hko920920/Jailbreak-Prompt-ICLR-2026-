interactions among A units. It is not an additive-unit model. Its incompatibility
with a finite observed table is not a statistical rejection or semantic causal

not that every completion fits. The independently reviewed frozen analyzer
already separates exact error, crossover witnesses and uncertainty bounds.

development evidence. These are not six independent replications; repeated
witnesses can share cells. The original45 cases are exposed, Llama was selected

Two simple capability tasks do not establish general utility. Independent
prespecified evidence remains necessary for a stronger paper claim; sealed
